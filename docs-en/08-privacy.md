# Privacy policy

[Português](../docs-pt-br/08-privacidade.md)

This policy covers the Cat game on every platform.

- The game does not collect personal data.
- The game does not send anything over the internet.
- The game has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.
- The game uses no analytics or tracking tools.
- The game stores only the chosen language, on the device itself. This data
  never leaves the device and is removed when the game is deleted.

Questions: open an
[issue on GitHub](https://github.com/RangelReale/kid-catgame-ai/issues).
