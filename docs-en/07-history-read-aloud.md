# The story of the cat game

[Português](../docs-pt-br/07-historico-para-ouvir.md)

This is the story of everything we did in the cat game, from the very
beginning.

## First day

First, we wrote down everything we wanted to do in the game.

Then the game opened for the first time. It was just an empty screen.

Then the cat showed up. At first, it was just a square. But it could
already walk and jump.

## Second day

The game went to the handheld video game, the Retroid.

The cat turned pink. It got paws, a tail, an eye and a mouth. Then its body
got the colors of the rainbow, and its head turned pink.

The cat learned to do a somersault when it jumps.

The level got really long, and the screen started moving along with the
cat.

The cat got wings! Now it flaps its wings in the air and goes higher, up to
three times.

In the sky, a smiling sun and three clouds appeared.

Three little monsters appeared. When the cat jumps on top of one, it
vanishes in a "poof" of sparkles. Later, it got easier to jump on top of
them.

At the end of the level, a flag appeared. It takes you to the next level.

The cat started going "boing" when it jumps.

## Third day

Buttons appeared on the screen, to play on the tablet without a
controller.

The game went to the iPad.

Level three: a beach, with the sea at the end.

Level four: the bottom of the sea. The cat became a mermaid and swims up
and down.

Level five: the cat comes out of the sea and goes into a room full of
computers.

Level six: an angry man runs after the cat. If he catches the cat, the
level starts again.

Level seven: a forest, with a little mouse in a flying saucer. At the end,
there is a big rat. Three jumps on top of it, and the flag appears.

Level eight: the cat flies in the sky and dodges the little birds.

Level nine: the cat comes down from the sky, from cloud to cloud, to the
grass.

Level ten: inside an airplane. The cat cannot bump into the suitcases.

The keyboard numbers started taking you straight to each level.

Level eleven: the airplane takes off, flies and lands by itself. A smiling
man picks the cat up.

Level twelve: a park with a pink castle and balloons.

Level thirteen: inside the castle, two princesses pick the cat up.

Level fourteen: in Brazil, the cat takes a photo with the princesses, and
the screen flashes.

Level fifteen: in the park, there is a giant monster. Five jumps on top of
it, and the flag appears.

Level sixteen: an amusement park! There is a Ferris wheel, trampolines, a
carousel of horses, a roller coaster, a tower that goes up and down and a
fishing game with little fish that jump out of the water.

And, last of all, it got easier to change levels with the keyboard.

## Fourth day

The game's video got new. Now it shows lots of levels: the mermaid, the big
rat, the sky, the airplane, the princesses, the giant monster, the Ferris
wheel and the roller coaster.

## Fifth day

The game got a start screen! It shows the name, "Cat Game", really big.
Behind it, the cat plays by itself in the levels, all in the color of an
old photo. You just press a button to play.

And when the cat finishes the last level, everything turns that same
color, "You won!" appears and the names of who made the game go up: Daughter
and Dad.

## What now?

The game already has sixteen levels. Four more to get to twenty!
